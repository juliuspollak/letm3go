## Household recording review

The iPhone recorder produces owner-scoped source activities and the app projects them through the existing grouping and backup path. Household automatic sharing publishes each member's permitted history separately. The shared Trips feed is a separate, lightweight social feed. The current Tesla reconciliation requires one Tesla source and rejects same-source pairs, so two iPhone recordings of the same household drive are still distinct. Changing the same-source predicate alone would be unsafe. The existing reconciler assumes a Tesla anchor, picks a route and combines metadata in a single-viewer projection. The household view must honor each source's sharing boundaries and keep each participant's data isolated. [App.jsx](https://github.com/juliuspollak/letm3go/blob/66bffba59c662cd4ef9ec3a6d5df90e181265608/src/App.jsx#L303) · [useSharedActivityFeed.js](https://github.com/juliuspollak/letm3go/blob/66bffba59c662cd4ef9ec3a6d5df90e181265608/src/lib/useSharedActivityFeed.js#L5) · [fleetJourneys.js](https://github.com/juliuspollak/letm3go/blob/66bffba59c662cd4ef9ec3a6d5df90e181265608/src/lib/fleetJourneys.js#L7)

### One physical journey, separate source recordings

- **Match only authorized finished sources.** Use shared calendar-day search as a cheap candidate step, then comparable time overlap, ordered route evidence, endpoints and known vehicle identity when available. Use distance between locations at comparable times and check route direction. Vehicle names and time overlap alone are insufficient. Different verified vehicles, parallel trips, convoys, brief partial overlaps, approximate routes or missing shared geometry should remain separate or show a private suggestion instead of silently linking. Live presence never supplies a historical participant.
- **Make linking non-destructive.** The Household view groups confidently matched, visible sources into one row and counts distance/duration once from one qualifying route. Keep the originals, their owners, photo/notes visibility and their individual tracking totals intact. Choose the best authorized route deterministically and preserve gaps. Do not let A–B and B–C imply A–C without compatible evidence across the group. Recompute when a source is deleted, unshared or late-syncs. A confirmed local link must be clearly marked device-only unless an authorized shared link store is available.
- **Keep provenance and people separate.** Record source owner, recording device/vehicle, and participant role as separate concepts. Use **Recorded by Alex’s iPhone** for device provenance, **Joined journey** only after participant confirmation or explicit self-attribution, **Driver unknown** by default, and **Driver · confirmed by Alex** only following an explicit claim allowed by the applicable sharing policy. Provide an undo or dispute path. Never infer driver or participant from the Tesla account, Collection owner, an iPhone recording alone, or household membership. Do not reveal roster membership or private recording existence through an inferred match.
- **Scope authority to the viewer.** Personal titles, photos, precise tracks and notes stay out of the shared card unless that source's current authorization allows them. Removing household access immediately removes the source from that viewer's grouped result. A household association never grants vehicle connection, edit, archive or deletion rights. Prefer existing shared-feed and route-access policies; a review is required before adding any cross-owner metadata or server-side matching.

### View design

| View | Top row | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal → Trips** | One row per personal journey. Show **Recorded by your iPhone** and a small **Shared journey** link only when an authorized household grouping exists. | List your route, your totals and your originals. Participant status is shown as confirmed or unknown. |
| **Household → Journeys** | One row for a high-confidence physical journey, with one distance/duration, confirmed participants and **Driver unknown** until explicitly assigned. Ambiguous rows remain separate. | Show authorized route, per-source attribution and **Confirm same journey** or **Keep separate** where permitted. Show only participants and roles this viewer is entitled to see. |
| **My Collection → Vehicle → Trips** | Filter existing trips by stable vehicle ID and show each linked physical journey once in the selected audience. | Show **Recorded by iPhone** or future **Recorded by vehicle**, the authorized participant list, and driver attribution if confirmed. A Tesla account connection does not add trips. |

Acceptance examples are two household members recording one car journey, two households on the same road in different cars, a convoy, two phones with one partial trace, a late sync, unsharing, delete/restore behavior, a driver claim dispute, and a member without access attempting to view the linked activity. Personal metrics must stay correct while Household counts the shared physical journey once.

## Verification and boundaries

The 57 focused tests passed across the PR's UI, credentials, fleet logic, local database and synthetic delivery suites. They do not establish real Tesla pairing, delivery or driving behavior, and do not cover cross-owner iPhone journey linking as a shipped feature. This review made no deployment, database, DNS, billing, TestFlight or PR changes.
